Terms of Service
The basics, in plain terms.
A short, human read on how AURA works — no dense legal language.
01
Purpose of AURA
AURA helps you notice, name, and reflect on how you're feeling through daily check-ins, mood cards, and gentle rituals. It's built to support self-understanding — not to replace professional medical or mental health care.
02
Acceptable Use
Use AURA for your own personal reflection and growth. Please don't use it to harass or impersonate others, or to try to access anyone else's account or data.
03
Account Responsibilities
You're responsible for keeping your Google sign-in secure and for the activity that happens under your account. Reach out if anything looks unfamiliar.
04
Content Ownership
Your entries, reflections, and generated cards belong to you. AURA doesn't claim ownership over anything you create or choose to share.
05
Service Availability
We work hard to keep AURA reliable, but occasional downtime, maintenance, or feature changes may happen as we continue improving the experience.
06
Updates
These terms may evolve as AURA grows. We'll keep any changes clear and reasonable — continuing to use AURA means you accept the current version.
07
Termination
You're free to stop using AURA and delete your account at any time. We may also suspend access if these terms are seriously or repeatedly violated.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Dog Avenger.

Dog Avenger (DogAvenger) is a community-driven token built on BNB Smart Chain, focused on transparency, holder rewards, ecosystem growth, and long-term value creation. It operates as a Community Takeover (CTO) with full community ownership.
Get a BSC-compatible wallet (Trust Wallet, MetaMask), purchase BNB, go to PancakeSwap, and swap BNB for DogAvenger using contract address 0x82ecE2bB9e1f3E0b19aFE141EF61C0745e28c75b. Set slippage to 2–5%.
Dog Avenger is built on the BNB Smart Chain (BSC), which offers fast, low-cost transactions and a large existing DeFi ecosystem.
Dog Avenger has a 2% buy tax and 1% sell tax. A portion of every transaction supports holder rewards through reflections and contributes to the ecosystem.
The 1% tax collected on transactions is redistributed proportionally to all holders. This means simply holding DogAvenger in your wallet earns you passive rewards with every buy, sell, or transfer that happens in the ecosystem.
